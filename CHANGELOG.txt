Android Wear SD CHANGELOG
=========================

V0.0.5 (20 August 2026) - Improved logging to help track down data transfer issues
V0.0.4 (20 August 2026) - Added Wakelock to try to stop the watch going to sleep, preventing data transfer at the required 5 second interval.
V0.0.3 (20 August 2026) - Increased data transfer period from 1 to 5 seconds to see if that is causing the battery drain problem.
V0.0.2 (18 August 2026) - Updated to use hardware batching of accelerometer data, and move calculations
                          into 1 second loop to try to improve battery life.
                        - Added OSD Icon
V1.0.0 (13 August 2026) - original by Dylan Griffiths, with minor modifications to make it compile and run with OSD phone app V5.0.
